# Soft Skills & Behavioral Interviews

Behavioral interviews focus on your past experiences to predict future behavior. The best way to answer these questions is using the **STAR Method**.

## The STAR Method

The STAR method is a structured manner of responding to a behavioral-based interview question by discussing the specific **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, and **R**esult of the situation you are describing.

### 1. Situation (S)
Describe the situation that you were in or the task that you needed to accomplish. You must describe a specific event or situation, not a generalized description of what you have done in the past. Be sure to give enough detail for the interviewer to understand. This situation can be from a previous job, a volunteer experience, or any relevant event.

### 2. Task (T)
What goal were you working toward? Describe the challenge, the expectations, and what needed to be accomplished.

### 3. Action (A)
Describe the actions you took to address the situation with an appropriate amount of detail and keep the focus on YOU. What specific steps did you take and what was your particular contribution? Use the word "I", not "we" when describing actions.

### 4. Result (R)
Describe the outcome of your actions and don't be shy about taking credit for your behavior. What happened? How did the event end? What did you accomplish? What did you learn? Make sure your answer contains multiple positive results.

## Common Behavioral Topics
- **Conflict Resolution:** "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker."
- **Leadership & Initiative:** "Describe a time you took the lead on a project."
- **Overcoming Obstacles:** "Tell me about a time you failed or made a mistake."
- **Adaptability:** "Describe a situation where you had to quickly adapt to a change."
